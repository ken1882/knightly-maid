# Knightly Maid

An add-on for **Touhou Little Maid** that puts your maid in the saddle.

## 🐉 Ride on your dragon *(with Dragon Survival)*

- Right click your maid with a **saddle** while you're a dragon, and she sits on your back.
- She stays put while you fly: no jumping off, no fall or suffocation damage.
- She's put down safely when you turn human, log out, die, change dimension, or after a crash.

## 🐎 Ride her own horse

- Carrying your maid? Right click a **horse, donkey, mule, skeleton or zombie horse** with a saddle, and she climbs on.
- She **gallops after you**. If you get too far away, she and her horse teleport to you.
- **Fights from horseback:** charges melee targets, and keeps her distance and circles when using bows, crossbows, danmaku, tridents, guns or spells.
- Her horse doesn't rear when hit. Wild horses don't buck her off.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Put her on your back (dragon) | Saddle → right click maid |
| Drop her off your back | Saddle → right click the air |
| Put her on a horse | Carry her, saddle → right click horse |
| Take her back | Saddle → right click horse or maid |
| Let her off the horse | Shift + right click her |

## 📦 Requirements

| Mod | |
|---|---|
| Touhou Little Maid | Required |
| Dragon Survival | Optional: dragon riding |
| Touhou Little Maid Spell, TACZ | Optional: spells and guns kite on horseback |

Install on **both client and server**. No Mixins.

## ⚠️ Known limits

- In combat she follows TLM's work schedule, and melee only reaches enemies right next to her.
- The horse can't teleport to you while you're flying.
- Saddle-clicking an unsaddled horse while carrying her puts her on it instead of saddling it.
